How it works

No drama. Just a receipt for every choice.

01

Scan locally

Cheepuru Katta inspects caches, app remnants, build artifacts, installers, and disk usage on your Mac. File names and contents never leave the device.

02

Review clearly

Results are grouped by category, size, risk level, and destination. Safe caches, recoverable Trash moves, and skipped protected files are separated.

03

Confirm deliberately

Nothing destructive runs from a scan. You choose what goes, confirm the action, then get a local receipt with what changed and what was refused.

Capabilities

Seven local tools. One careful interface.

Everything starts with preview data and ends with a local receipt. Power stays visible; risk stays bounded.

Clean

Review caches, logs, temporary files, browser clutter, AI-tool leftovers, and developer build artifacts before removal.

Uninstall

Remove apps and their support files, preferences, launch agents, login items, Dock entries, and safe remnants.

Optimize

Run safe maintenance tasks like rebuilding indexes, flushing DNS, pruning logs, and refreshing macOS services.

Analyze

Explore disk usage, drill into heavy directories, and move one-off folders to Trash with confirmation.

Status

Watch CPU, memory, disk, network, battery, thermals, uptime, health score, and top processes.

Purge

Clean project artifacts like dependency caches and build outputs, with project-boundary safeguards.

Installer Cleanup

Find old DMGs, PKGs, ZIPs, and redundant installers in common download locations before deleting.

Safety model

The app is allowed to be useful. It is not allowed to be sneaky.

Cheepuru Katta treats cleanup as a consent workflow, not a background ritual. The broom is gentle on purpose: your files matter more than a dramatic storage number.

01

Nothing uploads

Scans run locally. No file contents, names, browser history, documents, or cleanup lists are transmitted.

02

Preview before action

Every destructive path goes through review and confirmation. No silent sweeps, no panic banners.

03

Refuse when unsure

System roots, credentials, iCloud, keychains, messages, and protected app state are skipped by design.

04

Receipts stay local

Each operation records what changed, what failed, and what was protected so you can reason about it later.

Questions

Straight answers, no tiny print.

Is Cheepuru Katta free?

Yes. The intended product model is free download with optional support. No license activation is required for v1.

Does it replace the Mole CLI?

No. Cheepuru Katta is a visual companion concept around local Mole workflows. The CLI remains the open-source base and attribution stays visible.

Will it run without Full Disk Access?

Yes, but deeper caches and app containers may be skipped. The app should explain what permission changes before asking for it.

Where does donation money go?

The support button will point to the creator page we configure later, such as Buy Me a Coffee, Ko-fi, GitHub Sponsors, or Razorpay.
